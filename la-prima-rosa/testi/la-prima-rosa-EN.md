# The First Rose

[Intro — Distant female voice, Gregorian atmosphere]
In one heart, two voices,
one called, the other kept silent.
Two desires, one soul,
neither yielded to the other.

[Verse I — Deep male baritone]
A rose bloomed beneath a brief light,
tossed by two winds.
Its roots held it in the earth,
its branches longed for heaven.
And when evening came,
there was still no peace between roots and branches.
What divides the heart,
time does not always resolve.

[Verse II — Female choir, delicate and distant]
Distant land, long silence,
an absent hand, an unheard voice.
And the name returned in another flower,
but that flower was not the first rose.
The same name, another life,
the same memory, another form.

[Verse III — Organ enters, majestic but restrained]
And like a note taken up an octave,
a mother's face reappeared, higher.
An immense house, a thousand doors,
a thousand children, one cross.
He who had become father of all
remained a son within his heart.
And among the columns he sought
the hand that time had taken away.

[Pre-Chorus — Male and female voices intertwine]
Mother in the flesh,
mother in the name,
a mother keeping watch in the temple.
Three faces,
one love.
Each mother gives what she has,
none takes another's place.

[Bridge — Six lines whispered, almost without instruments]
What divides the heart does not die with time,
it returns in another form, at another moment.
Life twice over, the call three times,
one name, one house, a distant love.
What the womb once gave is never lost,
and what is lost can be entrusted.

[Chorus — Sacred polyphony, profound crescendo]
Not a return, but a memory.
Not an emptiness, but trust.
Not in her place, but beside.
Not possession, but love.
What you seek is not lost,
what you love lives in God.
One mother, an eternal memory,
and the Church, a mother too.

[Final Verse — Solo baritone, intimate and fragile]
And the ancient road once more led
to the land where silence remained.
But the traveler was not the same,
nor was the longing the same.
Not everything we lose
returns in another form.
But all that we have loved
is kept safe in God.

[Outro — Female voice, then male voice, complete silence]
Mother, remain in love.
Church, remain in truth.
What was, was.
Let what is, be.
[Long silence]
Mother…
and peace.
Amen.
